About Our Partnership

Building sustainable bridges between talent and tech opportunities.

The Anish Jadhav Memorial Foundation (AJMF) is proud to collaborate with NavGurukul, a visionary non-profit organization dedicated to bringing higher education and tech careers to youth from underprivileged backgrounds.

Through this alliance, AJMF and NavGurukul combine residential infrastructure, community outreach, mentorship, and intensive software engineering training to offer a completely fully-funded learning journey for aspiring students.

When Our Partnership Began

Partnership Since: 2022

Our joint initiative began with a shared objective: removing financial and geographic barriers so talented youth from low-income communities can master programming, soft skills, and professional leadership.

Why AJMF Partnered With NavGurukul

A shared mission to transform lives through digital literacy and employment.

Digital & Tech Skills

Providing hands-on software development training in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern web frameworks.

Expanding Access

Reaching youth who lack access to expensive private coaching or conventional university degrees.

Career Readiness

Preparing students with interview coaching, portfolio projects, and direct placement support.

About NavGurukul

  • Residential technology learning campuses
  • Self-learning and peer-to-peer pedagogy
  • Comprehensive coding & English curriculum
  • Hiring partner networks for job placements
